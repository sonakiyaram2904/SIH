ABOUT GEOGUARD NER

Technology for a safer Northeast

GeoGuard NER is an AI-driven landslide early warning concept developed to support disaster preparedness across India's North Eastern Region.

By bringing together geospatial information, environmental observations and predictive analytics, the platform aims to help communities and authorities make informed decisions before hazardous events occur.
